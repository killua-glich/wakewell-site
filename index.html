Common questions

How does WakeWell choose wake-up times?
Sleep happens in cycles of about 90 minutes. WakeWell calculates times that land at the end of a cycle, so you wake up lighter and more rested instead of being pulled out of deep sleep.

What do the three options do?
Sleep Now shows the best times to wake if you go to sleep right now. Wake Up At... tells you when to go to bed for a time you pick. Take a Nap times a power nap or a full recovery nap.

My alarm did not go off.
Check that notifications are allowed for WakeWell in iOS Settings, that your phone is not in a Focus mode that silences it, and that the volume is up. If it still happens, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

WakeWell does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, usage data, or any other information.

What stays on your device

Your alarms and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Third parties

WakeWell does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and it does not send data to any third-party service. There are no accounts to create.
